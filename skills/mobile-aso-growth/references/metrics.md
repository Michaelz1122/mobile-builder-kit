# Growth metrics and experiment outline

## Core metrics

- **CPI** = ad spend ÷ attributed installs (only when attribution is credible)
- **CPA** = ad spend ÷ attributed target actions
- **Store conversion rate** = installs ÷ eligible store listing visitors (define reporting source and denominator)
- **D1/D7 retention** = returned users at day 1/7 ÷ eligible install cohort (define event/cohort method)
- **ARPDAU** = revenue ÷ daily active users (identify revenue type)
- **Payback** = period until cumulative attributable margin offsets acquisition cost (state costs and uncertainty)

## Guardrails

- Do not claim keyword search volume is known without a reliable data source.
- Do not treat Meta platform installs as the same thing as Play Console installs without validating attribution and dates.
- Do not mix gross Google Play billing revenue with AdMob revenue without accounting for fees, refunds, taxes and reporting delays.
- Do not promise profit, ranking or downloads.
