# Mobile ASO & Growth

## Workflow

1. Ask for target country/language, app value proposition, competitors, budget and available actual Play Console/analytics data. Do not fabricate rankings, search volumes or installations.
2. Build a prioritized keyword list from defensible evidence; mark hypothesis keywords if no data is available.
3. Improve app title, short description, full description, screenshots and feature graphic within current Google Play limits. Avoid keyword stuffing and misleading claims.
4. Define conversion events and tracking validation *before* running app campaigns; do not assume SDK events are configured from a package name alone.
5. For Meta campaigns, choose an appropriate app objective, verify eligible app/account/event integration, set a small test budget, and evaluate creative and country cohorts honestly.
6. Report **CPI, cost per action, retention, ad revenue, IAP net revenue and payback** where data exists; distinguish estimated, observed and attributed metrics.
7. Reprioritize based on real results rather than anecdotes or fake growth numbers.
