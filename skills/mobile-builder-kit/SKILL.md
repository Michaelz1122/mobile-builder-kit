# Mobile Builder Kit — workflow orchestrator

Use the relevant installed sibling skills as needed, rather than copying all their content:
- \`app-discovery\`: focused product requirements interview (the custom Grill Me alternative).
- \`arabic-rtl\`: chat readability and real app RTL testing.
- \`app-blueprint\`: realistic MVP, acceptance criteria, one initial master coding prompt.
- \`play-in-app-updates\`: Google Play native update planning (not Expo OTA).
- \`play-in-app-review\`: user-friendly review timing and Play quota constraints.
- \`mobile-quality-audit\`: performance, security, accessibility and data integrity.
- \`play-launch-checklist\`: signing, Play testing, privacy and launch.
- \`mobile-aso-growth\`: ASO, analytics and measured growth.

## How to work

1. Inspect existing files, versions, platform targets and the user's constraints.
2. Choose the smallest useful MVP. Don't add accounts, cloud services or permissions by default.
3. Where requirements are uncertain, use app-discovery one question at a time.
4. Produce an implementable plan and one build prompt when requested.
5. Test the generated result and fix failures iteratively.
6. For native Play features, confirm compatibility and use an appropriate native build/test environment.
7. Do not claim unverified functionality or guaranteed store approval, reviews or RTL rendering.
8. Ask for approval before destructive changes, paid services, publishing and security-sensitive operations.

Use natural Egyptian Arabic when requested; never force raw HTML wrappers without a verified renderer.
